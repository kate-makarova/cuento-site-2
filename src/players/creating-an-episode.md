<p class="docs-lead">An episode is the core unit of play in Cuento — a thread with a cover image, participant list, in-game date, and location. Any member of a subforum can create one.</p>

## 1. Open the episode creation form

Navigate to the game subforum you want to post in and click **Create episode** in the top-right corner of the page.

![](https://i.ibb.co/Fb5bfFGv/upload.png)

A blank form will open.

![](https://i.ibb.co/RTn9Vnff/upload.png)

The **Date** field is disabled until you add characters.

## 2. Fill in the title and add characters

Enter the episode title in the **Title** field.

Add participating characters using the autocomplete in the **Characters** field: start typing a name, select the character from the dropdown, and click **Add character**.

![](https://i.ibb.co/JMtQSpR/upload.png)

Repeat for each participant. All added characters appear in the list below the input.

![](https://i.ibb.co/gZsbNs4s/upload.png)

## 3. Upload and crop a cover image

Click **Choose File** in the **Image** field and select an image from your device. After uploading, a crop interface opens — frame the area you want and click **Crop and upload**.

![](https://i.ibb.co/k2YkFLyT/upload.png)

## 4. Set the date

Once characters are added, the **Date** field becomes active. The available date formats are determined by the fandoms of the participating characters and are configured by administrators.

![](https://i.ibb.co/wFKkjCth/upload.png)

A single fandom may have multiple date formats. For example, Forgotten Realms dates can look like *1492 DR, Eleint 20* (with a day number) or *1492 DR, Midsummer* (a holiday without a day number). Pick the format that fits your scene.

![](https://i.ibb.co/Q7DFvG4h/upload.png)

## 5. Fill in the location and description

Enter the scene's setting in plain text in the **Location** field.

Add a short description or epigraph in the **Description** field. Use the toolbar above the field for formatting.

![](https://i.ibb.co/bgsFWLwD/upload.png)

## 6. Set the content rating

At the bottom of the form, rate the episode across three dimensions: **Language**, **Violence**, and **Sexual content**.

If you are unsure which rating applies, click **Help** — a table opens with detailed descriptions of each level.

![](https://i.ibb.co/ksZHwDXz/upload.png)

If any dimension is rated **3**, every reader (including guests) will see a content warning before they can read the thread.

![](https://i.ibb.co/vCTR7r6r/upload.png)

## Done

Click **Submit** — the episode is created. You will see the finished page with the cover image, participants, date, location, and description.

![](https://i.ibb.co/ZRBFPV6r/upload.png)
