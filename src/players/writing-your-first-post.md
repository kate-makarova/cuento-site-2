<p class="docs-lead">Posts are written in BBCode, a lightweight markup language designed for rich forum text.</p>

## The editor

The post editor includes a toolbar for common formatting: bold, italic, spoiler tags, alignment, and more. You can also type BBCode directly — tags like `[b]bold[/b]`, `[i]italic[/i]`, and `[spoiler]hidden text[/spoiler]`.

## Selecting a character

Before submitting, choose which character (or mask) is making the post. This attaches the character's profile to the post and shows their avatar in the thread.
