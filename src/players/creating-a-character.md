<p class="docs-lead">Characters are first-class objects in Cuento. Each character has a profile, custom fields, an avatar gallery, and optional masks for playing different personas.</p>

## Character profiles

Go to your account menu and choose Create Character. Fill in the name, backstory, and any custom fields your forum's admin has defined — these might include age, occupation, abilities, or anything specific to your game's setting.

## Masks

Masks let you play the same character under a different appearance or alias within a topic — useful for disguise scenes or unreliable narrator arcs. Create masks from your character's profile page.
