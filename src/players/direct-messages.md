<p class="docs-lead">Direct messages in Cuento are end-to-end encrypted. Only you and your recipient can read them.</p>

## How encryption works

When you first access the DM section, Cuento generates a key pair in your browser. Your private key never leaves your device. Messages are encrypted with the recipient's public key before being sent to the server.

## Losing your key

If you clear your browser data or switch devices without exporting your key, your past messages become unreadable. Export and back up your private key from your account settings before switching devices.
