<p class="docs-lead">Welcome to Cuento. This guide walks you through your first steps on a play-by-post forum running Cuento.</p>

## Creating an account

Register using the sign-up form on your forum's homepage. After confirming your email, you can log in and start exploring. Your administrator may need to grant you access to specific subforums before you can post.

## Navigating the forum

The main page shows the subforums you have access to. Each subforum contains topics — threaded conversations where the story unfolds post by post.
