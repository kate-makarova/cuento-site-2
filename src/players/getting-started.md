<p class="docs-lead">This guide walks you through joining a Cuento forum for the first time — from browsing open roles to having a registered account, a saved recovery code, and a character ready to play.</p>

## Browse the wanted characters

Before registering, explore the forum as a guest. Most Cuento forums publish a list of **wanted characters** — role descriptions that admins or existing players have written to recruit someone for a specific character concept. Each entry describes who the character is, what faction they belong to, and how they relate to the existing cast.

Find the wanted characters section in the forum's navigation and read through the listings. When you find a role you want to play, you can claim it immediately — even without an account.

## Claim a wanted character

Each wanted character listing has a **Claim** button. Clicking it reserves the role for you for **5 days** (3 days if you haven't registered yet). No approval from an admin is required — the slot is yours the moment you click.

During those 5 days you need to register and create a character sheet. If the window expires without a character sheet being submitted, the listing becomes available for others again. You can also release your claim early if you change your mind.

## Register

Cuento does not require an email address. Registration only needs a username and a password.

Go to the registration page (linked in the site header) and choose a username. This is the name other players see on your account — pick something you are comfortable being identified by, as it cannot be changed later without administrator help.

After submitting the form you are logged in immediately.

## Save your recovery codes

Immediately after your first login, Cuento generates a **key pair** used to encrypt your direct messages end-to-end. As part of this setup the system creates a set of **recovery codes** and shows them to you once.

**Write these down and store them somewhere safe.** Recovery codes are the only way to access your direct messages if you lose your password, and the only way to erase your personal data if you are ever locked out of your account. If you lose both your password and your recovery codes:

- An administrator can reset your password, but doing so without the original keys permanently destroys all your previous DMs.
- No one — not administrators, not the developers — can recover the messages. The encryption is designed so only you hold the keys.

You can export and regenerate recovery codes from your account settings at any time while you are still logged in.

## Create your character sheet

With an account and a live claim, go to your account menu and select **Create character**. The form includes:

- **Name** — the character's in-game name, not your username.
- **Custom fields** — each forum defines its own fields: background, appearance, faction, abilities, or anything specific to the game's setting. Fill in what is marked required; optional fields can be completed later from the character's profile page.
- **Avatar** — an image representing your character. You can upload one now or add it later.

When you submit the character sheet, it is linked to your claim record and the wanted character listing is marked as filled. Your character now appears in the forum's character list and can be added to episodes.
