<p class="docs-lead">This guide walks you through joining a Cuento forum for the first time — from browsing open roles to having a registered account, a saved recovery code, and a character ready to play.</p>

## Browse the wanted characters

Before registering, explore the forum as a guest. Most Cuento forums publish a list of **wanted characters** — roles that game masters or other players are looking to fill. Each entry describes a character concept: their place in the story, personality notes, relationships with existing characters, and sometimes specific requirements for the player.

Find the wanted characters section in your forum's navigation. Read through the entries and find a role that interests you. When you are ready to claim one, you will need an account first.

## Register

Cuento does not require an email address. Registration only needs a username and a password.

Go to the registration page (usually linked in the site header) and choose a username. This is the name other players will see on your account. Pick something you are comfortable being identified by — it cannot be changed later without administrator help.

After submitting the form you are logged in immediately.

## Save your recovery codes

Immediately after your first login, Cuento generates a **key pair** for end-to-end encryption of your direct messages. As part of this, the system creates a set of **recovery codes** and shows them to you once.

**Write these down and store them somewhere safe.** Recovery codes are the only way to regain access to your direct messages if you lose your password. If you lose both your password and your recovery codes:

- An administrator can reset your password, but resetting it without the original keys permanently destroys all your previous DMs.
- No one — not administrators, not the developers — can recover the messages for you. The encryption is designed so that only you hold the keys.

You can also export and regenerate recovery codes later from your account settings, as long as you are still logged in.

## Book a wanted character

Return to the wanted character you chose. Each listing has a **Book** or **Apply** action. Clicking it notifies the game masters that you want to play this role. Some forums require a written application; others confirm the booking immediately. Check the listing for any instructions.

Once your booking is accepted, the wanted character entry links to your account and other players can see the role is taken.

## Create your character sheet

After your booking is confirmed, you need to create the character's profile. Go to your account menu and choose **Create character** (or follow the link from the wanted character page if one is provided).

The character creation form includes:

- **Name** — the character's in-game name, not your username.
- **Custom fields** — each forum defines its own set of fields for the character sheet: background, appearance, faction, abilities, or anything specific to the game's setting. Fill in what is marked required; optional fields can be completed later.
- **Avatar** — an image representing your character. You can upload one now or add it later from the character's profile page.

Once submitted, your character appears in the forum's character list and can be added to episodes.
