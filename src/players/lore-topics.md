<p class="docs-lead">A lore topic is a wiki-style reference page inside a subforum. Instead of a linear thread, it has a navigation sidebar linking to individual pages — perfect for faction histories, in-universe documents, worldbuilding references, or any content that benefits from organized structure.</p>

## Creating a lore topic

If the subforum allows it, you will see a **Create lore topic** button in the subforum header alongside the buttons for creating regular topics and episodes. Click it to open the creation form.

Fill in:
- **Title** — the name of the lore article
- **Content** — the body of the first page (this becomes your Index page)
- **Stick the first post** (optional) — keeps the first post pinned at the top when browsing through post history

Click **Create**. The topic opens immediately with the first post as the Index page.

---

## The Index page

When a lore topic is created, the system automatically creates the first navigation entry and names it **Index**. This entry points to the first post you wrote.

The Index page is permanent:
- It cannot be deleted from the navigation
- It cannot be hidden
- It can be renamed if you want to give it a more descriptive title

The Index is what visitors see when they open the lore topic for the first time.

---

## Reading a lore topic

Lore topics open with a **navigation sidebar** on the left. Each item in the sidebar is a named page. Click any item to load that page's content in the main area.

External link entries open in a new browser tab rather than loading content inline.

---

## Page mode

Lore topics open in **page mode** by default: the sidebar navigation is prominent, and the content area shows one page at a time. This is different from a regular topic where you scroll through all posts chronologically.

You can still view the topic as a regular thread (for editing or browsing post history) by switching to thread view, but most readers will stay in page mode.

---

## Editing the navigation

To add, reorder, rename, or hide pages, open the topic and click **Edit navigation**. This link appears if you have editing rights on the topic.

The navigation editor shows all posts in the topic. For each post you can:
- **Assign a name** — this is the label that appears in the sidebar
- **Set the display order** — drag or enter a number to control where the item appears in the nav list
- **Hide** — temporarily remove a page from the sidebar without deleting the post. Hidden pages are still part of the topic but not visible to readers until you un-hide them

Posts that have no navigation entry yet appear in the editor so you can add them when they are ready.

---

## Adding new pages

To add a new page to an existing lore topic, post in the topic as normal. New posts do not automatically appear in the navigation — go to **Edit navigation** and assign a name and order to the new post to make it visible.

This lets you draft multiple posts and then publish them to the navigation when they are ready.

---

## External links in the navigation

In the navigation editor you can create entries that are not tied to any post — just a name and a URL. These appear in the sidebar like regular pages but open the linked URL in a new tab.

This is useful for linking to related topics, external resources, or other parts of the forum from within the lore article's navigation.
