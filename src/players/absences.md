<p class="docs-lead">If you know you will be unavailable for a period of time, you can register an absence. This pauses the auto-archiving countdown for all of your active characters and notifies your scene co-writers that you are away.</p>

## What an absence does

An absence is registered against your **user account**, not against individual characters. When an absence is active:

- The auto-archiving inactivity timer is extended for every one of your active characters by the number of days you are absent
- Your co-participants in active scenes receive a notification that you are away and when you plan to return
- After the absence ends, all your active characters receive an additional **3-day grace period** so you have time to catch up before archiving resumes

You do not need to do anything character-by-character — a single absence record covers your entire account.

---

## Registering an absence

Go to **Absences** (linked from your profile or the main navigation). The page shows all currently active absences on the forum — including other players' — so everyone knows who is away.

Click **Add Absence** and fill in:

- **Start date** — the first day you will be unavailable (can be today or a future date)
- **End date** — the day you plan to return

The end date cannot be in the past. The maximum duration of a single absence is set by your forum's administrator (typically 30 days). There is also a minimum cooldown between consecutive absences (typically 7 days after your last absence ended), so absences cannot be stacked back-to-back indefinitely.

---

## Cancelling an absence

If you return early, open the Absences page and click **Cancel** next to your active absence. The archiving timers for your characters are recalculated immediately.

---

## Absence visibility

All active absences are publicly visible on the Absences page. Anyone — including guests — can see who is currently away and when they are expected back. Administrators can also create an absence on behalf of any user if needed.
