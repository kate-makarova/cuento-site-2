<p class="docs-lead">If your forum's administrator has enabled AI features, you have access to a narration assistant and a lore Q&A tool.</p>

## Narration assist

When writing a post, click the AI button in the toolbar to open the narration assistant. Describe what you want to happen and the assistant will draft prose you can edit and post as your own.

## Lore Q&A

The lore assistant can answer questions about your game world using the forum's own content as its source. Ask it about characters, locations, or past events and it will search the archive to give you a grounded answer.
