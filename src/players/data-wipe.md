<p class="docs-lead">If you want to permanently erase your personal data from a Cuento forum, you can do so using a recovery code — no login required. This works even if you have been banned or an administrator has changed your password.</p>

## What gets deleted

The wipe permanently removes:

- All your **direct messages** (DMs)
- Your posts in **general (out-of-character) topics** — announcements, introductions, off-topic boards, and any other non-game thread

## What gets anonymised, not deleted

The following content is part of the shared story. It is not deleted — your authorship is severed and the records are reassigned to an anonymous system account:

- Your **game posts** (posts inside episodes)
- Your **character sheets** and profiles
- Your **wanted character entries**
- Your **lore posts**

This is intentional: removing in-character content would break the narrative for everyone else who participated in those episodes. The content stays; your name does not.

## How to wipe your data

1. Go to your account settings and find the **Data Wipe** section. If you are not logged in — because you were banned or your password was changed — navigate directly to the wipe page via the link in the site footer or at `/user/wipe`.
2. Enter one of your recovery codes.
3. Confirm the action.

That's it. The system verifies the recovery code, runs the deletion and anonymisation, and then deletes your user account. The action completes in seconds.

## This cannot be undone

Once you submit a recovery code for a wipe, the deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no grace period, no confirmation email, and no way to restore deleted messages or re-link anonymised content to a new account. The cryptographic connection between your identity and that content no longer exists after the wipe.

## If you have lost your recovery codes

If you no longer have your recovery codes and cannot log in, you cannot self-serve a wipe. Contact the forum administrator and ask them to run an admin-initiated wipe on your account. An admin-initiated wipe performs the same deletion and anonymisation — the difference is that it does not require a recovery code from you.

If you are still logged in but have lost your recovery codes, go to your account settings and regenerate them before logging out or switching devices.
