<p class="docs-lead">Cuento supports push notifications — the same kind you get from messaging apps and banks. They let you receive alerts about new posts and messages directly on your phone's lock screen.</p>

## Step 1 — Install the forum as a PWA

Notifications only work when the forum is installed as a standalone app (PWA) on your phone.

**On iPhone:**

1. Open the forum in **Safari** (not Chrome or another browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

The forum will appear on your home screen as a separate icon. Always launch it through that icon, not through the browser.

**On Android:**

Open the forum in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner, and select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Step 2 — Enable notifications in settings

1. Open the forum through the home screen icon.
2. Go to your user settings.
3. Tap **Enable push notifications**.
4. Grant the permission your phone requests.

That's it — notifications are now active.

## Important details

- **Each device must be enabled separately.** If you have three phones and enabled notifications on one, the other two will not receive them.
- **Notifications only arrive when you are offline.** If the forum is open in any browser tab on your computer, nothing will be sent to your phone.
