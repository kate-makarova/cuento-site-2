<p class="docs-lead">Some forums run an optional currency system that rewards you automatically for activity and lets you spend what you earn on in-forum benefits. If your forum has this feature enabled, your balance is shown in your profile and in the navigation bar.</p>

## How currency works

Currency is **per user account** — not per character. All your characters share one balance.

**Income is automatic.** You do not need to claim rewards; they are added to your balance when the qualifying action happens. The most common source is writing episode posts — each post earns a set amount. Other events that may earn currency include having a character approved or creating a wanted character listing. Additionally, the forum may award one-time bonuses when you reach certain post-count milestones.

Which income events are active and how much each pays is configured by the administrator — check the [Store](#the-store-page) page to see the current rates on your forum.

**Spending is manual.** You choose when and what to spend your balance on. Currently the main use is purchasing auto-archiving immunity for a character — protecting it from being archived due to inactivity for a set period. The purchase button appears on the character's profile page.

## The Store page

The **Store** (`/shop`) is a reference page showing:

- **Income types** — what activities earn currency and how much each pays
- **Spend types** — what can be purchased and at what cost

This page is informational — purchases are made directly from the relevant context (such as a character's profile page), not from the Store itself.

---

## Transaction history

Your full currency history is at **Profile → Transactions**. Each entry shows:

- The type of transaction (which income event or spend action)
- The amount, with a `+` for income and `−` for spending
- The date
- A link to the relevant post or character where applicable

Admins can view and add manual transactions to any user's history (for example, to issue a bonus or correct an error).
