<p class="docs-lead">The statistics page gives everyone — including guests — a live view of forum activity. It shows six summary numbers and five charts covering posts, episodes, characters, and faction activity for any time period you choose.</p>

## Time period

Five presets are available at the top of the page:

| Preset | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Week to date | Monday of the current week through today |
| Month to date | The 1st of the current month through today |
| Last week | The previous Monday–Sunday |
| Last month | The full previous calendar month |
| Custom | A date range you pick yourself |

The default on load is **Month to date**. All periods are aligned to your local timezone — the page sends your UTC offset to the server so the day boundaries match your clock, not the server's.

## Summary cards

Six numbers appear at the top, each scoped to the selected period:

- **New Episodes** — episodes opened during the period
- **Finished Episodes** — episodes that reached finished status
- **Inactivated Episodes** — episodes that were archived
- **Total Posts** — game posts written (episode posts only, not off-topic discussions)
- **Wanted Characters** — wanted character listings created
- **Accepted Characters** — characters whose sheets were accepted and became active

## Charts

### Writing Activity

A line chart with one point per day across the full period. Days with no posts show as zero — the line never has gaps. Month names appear on the first of each month; other days show the day number.

### Top Writers

A horizontal bar chart of the 10 most active writers by game post count for the period.

### Top Characters

A horizontal bar chart of the 10 most active characters by game post count. Masks (alternate identities a character can post under) appear as their own entries, separate from the character they belong to.

### Episodes by Faction

A stacked horizontal bar chart, one bar per faction. Each bar has three segments: **Created** (green), **Finished** (blue), **Archived** (red). An episode is counted under a faction if any of its participating characters belongs to that faction. Factions with no activity in the period are not shown.

### Posts by Faction

A vertical bar chart of post count per faction. Only posts written **as a character** are counted here — GM posts and posts written as a user (without a character profile) are excluded.

## Who can see this

The statistics page is fully public. No account is required to view it, and everyone sees the same data.
